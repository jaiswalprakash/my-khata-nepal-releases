    Questions before you install.

    Does it need the internet?

    No. Khata, bills, reports and printing work offline. The internet is used only for updates and the optional Google Drive backup.

    Which computers does it run on?

    64-bit Windows 10 or later, and Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel chips. Windows 7, 8 and 8.1 are not supported.

    My computer warns me before opening the app. Is that normal?

    Yes, for now. The installers are not yet signed with a paid certificate. On Windows choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. On Mac, right-click the app and choose Open, or approve it in Privacy & Security.

    How do updates work?

    On Windows, the app downloads updates in the background and offers “Restart now”. On Mac it shows “Update available” and opens the download page. Back up before updating.

    Is my data encrypted?

    Google Drive backups are encrypted. The data file on your computer is not, so keep your computer protected and your backups current.

    What is not included yet?

    Sync between computers, mobile apps, stock management, Excel export and IRD CBMS integration. Tax invoices use a basic IRD layout without online sync.
